• Conducted a combination of Molecular Statics (MS), MD and Monte Carlo (MC) types of simulations in
LAMMPS to investigate the elemental GB segregation behavior of HEAs.

• Explored the effect of misorientation of asymmetric tilt GB and thermal segregation of HEAs.

• Identified the positive impact of elemental segregation around GBs, which can be tailored to design and produce
new HEAs with advanced mechanical properties.
