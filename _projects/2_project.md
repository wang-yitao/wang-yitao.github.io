• Developed a high-performance mesoscale phase-field (PF) model incorporating point defect dynamics to simulate
microstructural evolution in Fe-Cr binary and Fe-Ni-Cr ternary alloys, utilizing Message Passing Interface (MPI)
in C for large-scale parallel computation.

• Investigated the effects of grain size, GB mobility, and irradiation conditions on RIS concentration profiles, and
validated simulation results through comparison with experimental data.

• Demonstrated the synergistic interplay of RIS and spinodal decomposition through PF simulations, yielding
results in qualitative agreement with experimental observations.

• Examined the interplay between GBs and radiation damage in influencing microstructural evolution in nuclear
environments.
